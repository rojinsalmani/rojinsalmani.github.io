I am an Electrical Engineering graduate from **Sharif University of Technology** (B.Sc., GPA: 18.54/20.00) and a **Remote Research Intern at Sabancı University**. My work sits at the intersection of wireless communications, mobile networks, and machine learning.

## Research Interests
* Next-generation wireless and THz-band aerial networks
* Machine learning and reinforcement learning for wireless channel modeling and routing
* 5G/6G radio access networks (RAN), open-source cellular testbeds, and network automation

## Current & Recent Research
* **Remote Research Intern, Sabancı University** *(Aug. 2026 – Present)*: Developing a Python evaluation framework with feature transformations for neural network THz channel models, benchmarking computational complexity for a journal submission, and building a simulation environment for deep-RL-based outage-aware 3D THz aerial routing.
* **Research Intern (PURE), Sabancı University** *(July 2026 – Aug. 2026)*: Reviewed broadband THz path loss modeling (0.1–1.0 THz) and implemented baseline physical regression models for atmospheric attenuation in aerial networks.
* **Undergraduate Research, Sharif University of Technology** *(Aug. 2025 – June 2026)*: Deployed an over-the-air standalone 5G testbed using USRP hardware, Open5GS, and srsRAN, and built a Regex/Drain3 preprocessing pipeline converting raw 5G logs into structured JSON for LLM-based root-cause analysis.
